Getting there

A sketch of the way, not to scale.

  • By roadFrom Mumbai, take the Samruddhi Expressway. Join it at Amane near Thane and drive up to Igatpuri through the new tunnel, skipping Kasara Ghat. Plan on about three hours, more with traffic, and follow our map pin for the last stretch.
  • By trainIgatpuri station is the closest, about 20 km from the resort, and most Mumbai–Nashik trains stop there, roughly two and a half to three hours from CSMT. Nashik Road has a few more trains but is farther out, about an hour by road. Cabs wait at both.
  • By airFly into Mumbai and drive up, about three to four hours. Nashik (Ozar) airport is closer, about an hour and a half by road, with fewer flights.

Questions

What do I need for check-in?
Just your ID. Send us a photo of a government ID before you travel and we'll have everything ready, so check-in is quick.
When should I reach on Saturday?
Aim to arrive by lunchtime. The Haldi starts at 2 PM, and you'll want time to settle in and change into yellow.
How cold will it get?
Afternoons are warm and sunny, around 30°C. After sunset it drops to about 15°C, so bring a shawl or a light jacket for the Sangeet and the early Vedic morning.
What should I wear to each event?
Yellow for the Haldi, something that dazzles for the Sangeet, and traditional wear for the Vedic wedding and the Varmala. See what to wear.
I haven't been to a Marathi wedding. Will I follow along?
Absolutely. There's a short guide in the Vedic wedding and Varmala sections, and someone will always be around to tell you what's happening.
How do I get there?
By road on the Samruddhi Expressway, about three hours from Mumbai, or by train to Igatpuri (closest) or Nashik Road. Directions and map.
What if I get lost on the way?
Open our map pin in Getting there for turn-by-turn directions to the resort.
